# Content Extraction Report

## Source availability

The requested source was `http://myanmarfishery.com/index.htm`. The domain did not resolve in the available browser and shell environments. An indexed mirror at `https://myanmarfishery.vercel.app/` was available and was used as the accessible content reference. The mirror identifies the historic site as “Myanmar Fishery & Livestock Online” and preserves pages associated with the 2003 Myanmar Fishery and Livestock Fair.

## Pages inspected

- `/index.htm` — homepage news, portal positioning, source objectives, and historical buying-lead labels.
- `/introduction.htm` — brief introduction covering Myanmar’s coastline, coastal swamps, marine fisheries, inland water bodies, aquaculture, livestock, domestic consumption, investment, and the 2003 fair.
- `/objectives.htm` — five objectives covering awareness, market access, foreign investment, technical know-how, and healthy value-added goods.
- `/generalinformation/index.htm` — event dates, hours, venue, organizers, participation fees, booth facilities, exhibitors, freight forwarding, remittance, advertising, and fair services.
- `/contact.htm` — Directorate of Trade and Department of Fisheries addresses, telephone numbers, fax numbers, and email addresses.
- `/member/index.htm` — site navigation, historical member directory entries, footer, forum copy, and buying-lead links.
- `/participants.htm` — 2002 participant list and booth references.
- `/freshwater-fish/index.htm`, `/marine/index.htm`, `/aquatic/index.htm` — linked in the source navigation but returned no extractable text during the research pass.
- `/mpeameeting_15dec07.htm` — linked as a news item but returned no extractable text during the research pass.

## Preserved in the redesign

The website preserves the source’s introduction and objective themes, the freshwater / marine / aquatic-fauna navigation vocabulary, historical fair information, organizer contacts, historical buying-lead labels, and clear historical-source caveats. It also retains the source’s distinction between sector information and active commercial claims.

The introduction includes the source figures of a 2,832-kilometer coastline, approximately 0.5 million hectares of coastal swamps, an estimated 1.05 million-ton marine maximum sustainable yield, approximately 8.2 million hectares of inland water bodies, and approximately seventy thousand tons of annual flood-fishery production. These are attributed to the historical source and are not presented as current statistics.

## Missing or uncertain information

The live source domain was unavailable. The detailed text for freshwater fish, marine fish, and aquatic fauna pages could not be extracted. The source did not provide a reliable current company profile, current product catalogue, current operating address, current staff contacts, or current service availability. Those gaps are not filled with invented information. Historical contact details are labelled for verification.

## Design and implementation note

The visual design, layout, navigation treatment, CSS, JavaScript, and page structure are new. The site is implemented with HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript only. Local image assets are documented separately in `IMAGE-SOURCES.md`.
