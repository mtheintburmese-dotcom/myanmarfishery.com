Brief introduction

A long coastline, deep inland waters, and room to grow.

Myanmar, a member of ASEAN, has a long coastline of 2,832 kilometers. The original source describes coastal swamps of about 0.5 million hectares and inland water bodies covering about 8.2 million hectares.

It presents aquaculture, flood fisheries and inland fisheries as opportunities for increasing fish production, while positioning the sector for domestic consumption, regional trade and investment.
