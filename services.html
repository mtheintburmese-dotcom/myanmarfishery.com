Trade infrastructure around the fair.

The preserved source is primarily an event and industry portal. Its general information page detailed exhibitor participation, logistics and visitor-facing services for the 2003 fair.

Fair details

Myanmar Fishery & Livestock Fair — 2003

Period: 31 October 2003 to 4 November 2003
Time: 09:00–17:00
Venue: Yangon Trade Centre, Upper Pazundaung Road, Yangon, Myanmar

Exhibitors

Who the source invited

Manufacturers, traders, sales representatives, agents, associations and public organizations in the fishery and livestock business from Myanmar and abroad.

On-site support

Services listed

  • Booth construction and decoration
  • Aluminum counter
  • Facsimile and telephone service
  • Daily cleaning and free electricity
  • Two free exhibitor passes per stand unit
  • Tour service counter
Logistics

Clearing and forwarding

NMT Ltd. was named as the official freight forwarder for customs clearance, transportation, on-site handling and re-export arrangements.
